Tau Motors develops software-defined power conversion systems and rare-earth-free electric powertrains for electric vehicles and industrial motor applications.

### pcb design & bench testing

*   **KiCad Layout & Debug:** Resolved routing and schematic errors for traction power electronics and testbench diagnostic circuit boards in KiCad.
*   **Assembly & Soldering:** Hand-assembled prototypes using surface-mount (SMD) and through-hole soldering.
*   **Dynamometer Validation:** Tested PCB system behaviors directly on high-voltage motor testbenches, streamlining component sourcing and fabrication workflows.

### inventory management

*   **System Architecture:** Architected Tau's centralized inventory management infrastructure by deploying open-source **InvenTree** backed by custom Python automation (`pandas`, `streamlit`).
*   **Workflow Optimization:** Cataloged hardware stock to eliminate component bottlenecks during rapid prototyping iterations.
