I recently graduated from Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) with my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering (May 2026) and am currently continuing my education in the ECE M.S. program (expected May 2027). During my undergraduate career, I was honored to receive the **CMU Senior Leadership Recognition Award** and was a 6-time **Dean's List** recipient.

My academic concentration centers on:
*   **Computer Architecture:** Designing pipelined processors, memory hierarchies, and accelerators.
*   **Digital VLSI/IC Design:** Translating logic layouts from RTL through synthesis, placement & routing, and verification to tapeout.
*   **Reconfigurable Computing:** Utilizing FPGAs and custom execution units to build highly accelerated hardware engines.

For a full semester-by-semester breakdown of all ECE, computer science, mathematics, and elective courses I have completed, see the [CMU List of Classes]({% link _posts/2026-02-11-cmu-classes.md %}).
